## The problem

Paylane lost almost half of new business accounts between sign-up and the first invoice. The flow asked for company documents before showing anything useful, and every rejected upload sent people back to the start.

## What I did

- Mapped the flow with support and compliance, and marked which checks the law needs before payouts and which only before the first payout.
- Moved the identity check after the first draft invoice, so people see the product working before the paperwork.
- Replaced the long form with a checklist that saves every step and explains each document in one sentence.
- Tested three prototypes with eight small-business owners; the checklist won on both speed and confidence.

> "I finally knew what was left and why." A tester after the second round.

## Outcome

The new flow shipped to all new accounts in March. The first invoice now comes four minutes after sign-up on the median, and the checklist pattern became part of the design system.
