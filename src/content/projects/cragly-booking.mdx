## The brief

Cragly runs six bouldering gyms. Members booked through a generic calendar tool, peak hours were overcrowded, and the front desk spent evenings answering "is it busy now?".

## Research

- Two weeks of desk logs: a third of all questions were about how full the gym was.
- Interviews with twelve members: regulars wanted a two-tap rebooking, newcomers wanted to know what to bring.
- A review of six booking apps for sports clubs, focused on how they show capacity.

## Design

The home screen leads with a live occupancy bar for each gym and the next free slots. Booking a session takes two taps for anyone with a pass; newcomers get a short checklist (shoes, chalk, waiver) before they pay.

## Result

Peak-hour crowding dropped once people could see it coming, and the desk got its evenings back. The component kit I built for the app is now used for the gyms' web booking too.
